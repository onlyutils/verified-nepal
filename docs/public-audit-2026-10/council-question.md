# Council question: what should the public side of Verified Nepal become for Nepali readers?

## Context (from seven research notes in research_notes/Nepali online news audience/, which you must read)

**The site.** Verified Nepal (verifiednepal.com) is a volunteer-run, bilingual (English / नेपाली), mobile-first disaster-response and mutual-aid platform for Nepal. Brand: "verified, not viral". Public surfaces today: a home page that is a task hub (hero + five action cards: get help, give help, find a person, drop centres, report incident; relief-goods band; flood-impact satellite map CTA; NDRRMA live counts hard-wired to the Aug 2026 Rasuwa/Bhote Koshi flood; relief map; OPMCM notices; stories strip; hotlines; PMDRF donate; chat CTA). Reading surfaces: /articles (signed-in block editor, mandatory sources, byline, Gregorian timestamp in NPT, 7 fixed tags, like/view counters, WhatsApp/Facebook/copy share), stories (photo/video + caption), /flood-impact (MapLibre satellite drive), /climate (compensation-not-sympathy messages to countries), /our-message, /projects, public masked /ledger and /audit. Everything is pre-moderated by district-scoped volunteer moderators. A "local updates" feature (post-moderated short local news from approved reporter-role users, 753 local levels, "Not yet checked / Checked by volunteer" labels, two labelled ad slots) is fully decided in docs/NEWS-PORTAL-DECISIONS.md but NOT built.

**Inventory facts (verified_nepal_public_site_inventory.md).** Production API returns zero articles, stories, projects, centres or incidents today: every reading surface renders an empty state. No RSS, newsletter, push, Viber share, article:published_time, NewsArticle schema, hreflang, Nepali URL, BS (Bikram Sambat) dates, comments, related items, corrections log, author pages, search across content, "your area" block, or disaster switcher. Fonts: Google Fonts Noto Sans + Noto Sans Devanagari at 4 weights (up to ~0.9 MB for a Nepali page). Entry JS 229 KB brotli on every page; Leaflet + Esri tiles on home; service worker precaches 124 files incl. a 1.5 MB rescued-persons JSON on first visit; www. not redirected. Operator disclosed only as "a single private individual"; GOVERNANCE and MODERATION-GUIDELINES exist in docs/ but are not linked publicly; no DoIB statement; the word "Verified" is used in five distinct senses and explained only on /how-to. No ad slots; all donate CTAs go off-site to the PM relief fund.

**Audience facts.** 16.6M internet users (56%), growth stalled; Android 75%; Facebook reaches ~90% of internet users and is the primary news source for 62% overall and 82.6% of 16-24s; YouTube 44%; Hamro Patro is "go-to" for 6%; TikTok only 1.4% as go-to despite reach. Nepali is mother tongue for 44.9%; literacy 76% (women 69%); Madhesh province reads online news far less (20% vs 70% Gandaki). Trust: 2-3% trust social media, ~8% trust online portals, ~one-third trust TV/radio/print, yet 67% saw misinformation last week. People DISCOVER on Facebook/YouTube and VERIFY on radio/TV bulletins. Evening peak 6-11 pm. Video-first shift; Sept 2025 ban of 26 platforms (TikTok/Viber exempt) led to Gen Z protests, Discord/Instagram organising, Kantipur offices burned. Diaspora ~3.8M (85% Gulf + Malaysia), remittances Rs 2.36T.

**Pain points.** Ads are the dominant complaint (four r/Nepal threads exist just to build ad blockers for Onlinekhabar et al; "news between ads"; Hamro Patro reviews dominated by unskippable ads and notification spam). App asks: dark mode, search, speed, broken Devanagari rendering, forced login. Content: clickbait headline-body mismatch (measured academically), copy-paste from wires (PCN blocked 15 portals 2023), political "setting"/झोले मिडिया, macro-political fixation, almost no local (palika) content. Trust: YouTube "news" channels with false thumbnails are the main fake-news vector; AI/recycled video waves in Sept 2025 and the Aug 2026 flood; fake PM-relief-fund QR codes within 48 h. Praise: Routine of Nepal Banda for speed, brevity, Roman-Nepali "friend" voice, verification before posting (9.5M followers); Hamro Patro for the utility bundle; Kathmandu Post/Setopati/Onlinekhabar for lowest misleading-news rates.

**Traction/business.** Onlinekhabar ~6.8M visits/quarter, 69-83% "direct" (app + dark social); Hamro Patro is search-led. 100 outlets closed and 243 journalists quit in 2023; arrears of 2-12 months common. Kantipur launched its first digital subscription Aug 2026 aimed at diaspora. Regulation: DoIB registration now Rs 5,000 + Rs 2,500/yr, requires a company with PAN, editor + 3 paid staff, registration number/editor names/privacy policy displayed, public corrections; 2017 directive makes operator liable for user comments; ETA s.47 used against journalists 2024-2026; Social Media Bill withdrawn Feb 2026. PCN "Humane disaster reporting guidelines 2083" (Sept 2026): verify deaths/missing with official sources, no faces of the dead, label AI imagery, immediate public corrections.

**Disaster info seeking.** Aug 2026 flood: 1,410 dead / 6,145 missing; alert via 600k SMS 38 min after; official info flowed through NDRRMA Facebook and PDF bulletins; a civil servant's GitHub dashboard beat the government missing-persons portal by days; volunteers said "there was no centralized database"; the state itself published corpse photos. Relief failures are geographic (wards unreached after 24 days, CDO stopped answering) and ward chairs wanted a public record proving relief had NOT arrived. Trust markers that work: provenance (original post, timestamp, bulletin number), institutional domain, verified badge; logos are forged. Loss-and-damage framing is "a refrain for every segment of Nepali society" but critics say it crowds out domestic accountability. Only 49% of households had heard of "climate change" (2016).

**UI/UX conventions.** Nepali portals: BS date masthead in Devanagari digits ("बुधबार, असोज २८, २०८३"), relative times ("११ घण्टा अगाडि"), Nepali category bar, "ताजा"/"भर्खरैको खबर", "धेरै पढिएका", reporter bylines with "तस्बिर :" credits, DoI registration number in footer; English editions are separate properties. Fonts in use: Mukta (Setopati, Ratopati), Martel (eKantipur), Inter (Onlinekhabar, Kathmandu Post). Agency guidance: 17-18 px body, 1.7-1.8 line-height, preload one Devanagari weight. Input is Romanized transliteration; search needs normalisation. Top viewports 414x896, 360x800; Chrome ~80%; data cheap (1.1 GB/Rs 50); Ncell claims 95% 4G. Dina Nepali TTS for TalkBack launched Dec 2024. MoCIT 2021 directive requires responsive, disability-friendly sites in Nepali Unicode + English.

## The question

Given this evidence, the owner wants three things judged:
1. What must an IDEAL Nepali public information/news portal look like to solve the audience, publisher, moderator, donor, NGO and local-government pain points above (UI/UX, content model, trust/verification display, distribution, language, dates, performance, governance, legal posture, monetisation)?
2. Where is Verified Nepal's PUBLIC site already aligned with that ideal, and where is it NOT (be concrete, cite inventory facts)?
3. What should the owner build, change or stop first, given one owner plus volunteers and a decided-but-unbuilt "local updates" feature?

What's at stake: the site's "verified, not viral" promise competes for the VERIFICATION role that radio/TV currently hold, in a market where 92% of readers distrust online portals, where empty reading surfaces undermine the promise, and where becoming a "news portal" triggers DoIB registration and operator liability. A wrong call wastes volunteer effort or exposes the owner legally.

## Correction added after round 2 (2026-10-14)

docs/NEWS-PORTAL-DECISIONS.md was updated while the council ran. Two premises above are now stale:
- **Moderation:** the owner REJECTED post-moderation. Local updates will be PRE-moderated (same pending -> published path as articles), with an own Desk queue, "reject if no source, no place, or no date", scope-based reviewer rules against friend-approves-friend, 5 submissions/24 h per reporter. "Not yet checked" labels, tiers and strikes are dropped.
- **DoIB:** the owner decided that VerifiedNepal itself registers as the online media outlet; reporters publish under that registration. Public naming stays "local updates / local correspondents" until registration is done. The doc notes its sources were law-firm summaries, not official text, and that fees, editor requirements and renewal must be confirmed with DoIB directly.
- Build order decided: (1) kind:news + Desk queue, (2) locality GSI + /local/<municipality> + "Now" strip, (3) reporter role, (4) ad item + two slots, (5) later: AI writer, condolences, ad-manager role. Only localities with a published item are listed anywhere.
Advisors C and E (and reviews 1, 2, 5) argued against post-moderation and against registering; the moderation point is moot, and the registration point becomes "can a volunteer operation meet the 2025 rules (company, PAN, editor, three bank-paid staff)?", which the publisher research note raises and the decisions doc flags as unconfirmed.
