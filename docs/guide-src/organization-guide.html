VERIFIEDNEPAL

Running Your
Organization

A guide for NGOs, community groups and other organizations — registering, opening drop centers where people can drop off help, and keeping a transparent goods ledger.

FOR ORGANIZATIONS

Register your organization

Any group helping on the ground can register — NGOs, community groups, companies, religious organizations or government teams. Anyone signed in with Google can register; moderators verify afterwards.

  1. Sign in with Google. The account you use becomes the organization's owner account.
  2. Fill in the registration form. Your organization's name and type, a contact person and phone, the districts you work in (up to 10), and a short description of what you do. Registration number and website are optional.
  3. Start working immediately. Your organization begins as provisional — you can create drop centers and log goods right away. They appear publicly, badged "Unverified", until a moderator verifies you.
  4. Find it again anytime. Your organization lives at verifiednepal.com/org — "My organization", also linked in the site footer.

How verification works

A moderator reviews every registration, usually with a phone call to your contact person. Verified organizations lose the "Unverified" badge and are shown under one of three tiers:

Known
On a seed list or an established, publicly known organization.
Vouched
An already-verified organization vouched for you. Your dashboard shows your organization ID — share it with a verified organization and ask them to vouch.
Self-declared
Moderators checked what they could; the organization's details are self-declared.
FOR ORGANIZATIONS

Open drop centers

A drop center is a physical place where anyone can drop off help — food, water, shelter goods and other supplies. Each center you create is listed publicly on the site's drop-center map and pages, so people nearby know where to bring things.

  • Set it up clearly. Name, district, address, opening hours, a contact phone, and which categories of goods you accept.
  • Keep the status honest. Mark a center Open, Paused or Closed as things change.
  • Print the donor QR poster. Donors scan it at your center — "I dropped something here" — declare what they left, and get a reference code.
  • Confirm donor drops. Declared drops wait in your dashboard; confirming one logs it as an intake, and donors can follow their goods with their code.
FOR ORGANIZATIONS

Keep the goods ledger

Every center has a running ledger of goods in and out. It takes seconds per entry, and it is what makes your work credible to donors and moderators.

  1. Intake — goods arriving at the center, whether from a confirmed donor drop or logged directly.
  2. Distribution — goods handed out to people who need them.
  3. Transfers — send stock to another drop center on VerifiedNepal, or record that it went somewhere else. The receiving center confirms the quantity that actually arrived; any difference is recorded as a discrepancy.
  4. Corrections — mistakes aren't edited away. A correction reverses the wrong entry with a reason, then you log the right one. The trail stays visible.

Everything is public. Stock on hand and recent activity are visible to everyone, marked as logged by your organization and not independently verified. An honest, current ledger is your reputation.

Bring in your team

  • Invite staff by email. From your dashboard, invite colleagues by their Google email — they join automatically the first time they sign in.
  • Staff can run centers. Staff log intakes, distributions and transfers; only the owner can edit the organization's details.
  • Vouch for others. Once you're verified, you can vouch for a pending organization you know and trust — once per organization.
