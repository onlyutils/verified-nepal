A simple guide to offering help — what you'll need, how matching works, and how handovers stay safe.

PROVIDING HELP

Browse real, checked needs

Every request you see has already been checked by a moderator, so you know it's genuine. You can filter by disaster, district or the kind of help needed — Goods, Shelter, Transport, Medical, Skilled labour, or Funds/guidance. To protect people's privacy, listings show only a general area, not a full name or contact details.

How to offer help

  1. Sign in with Google. A quick, one-time step so your offer can be tracked reliably.
  2. Fill in a short form. What you can offer, and which district(s) you can help in. Helping on behalf of a group or organization? You can note that too.
  3. Submit it. You'll get a reference code confirming your offer has been received.
  4. A moderator matches you. They'll pair your offer with a verified request that fits what you can give.
  5. Handover happens safely, in person. A moderator checks a claim code at the point of handover — so you're never just handing things to a stranger with no verification.

Helping through a verified organization? Staff of a verified organization can also claim a published need directly from the board and arrange the handover themselves, without waiting for a moderator match. See the Organization guide.

See something wrong?

  • Report it directly — every listing has a "Report a problem" option: already received aid, not a real need, or other. No sign-in required.

Safety built in. All handovers of aid go through a moderator check, so you're never asked to exchange personal contact details directly with someone you can't verify.
