Drop centers → https://verifiednepal.com/drop-centers
Verified collection points where anyone can hand in relief goods. Each one lists what it needs right now, its hours and a phone number. This is the answer to "where do I take things?"

Flood impact → https://verifiednepal.com/flood-impact
Satellite imagery of the river corridor before and after the flood, place by place, from the glacial lake down through Trishuli Bazaar and Devighat to Chitwan. Open data, so nobody has to take anyone's word for it.

No brand, no fundraising, no money passes through the site. It is free and will stay free.

If your organisation collects relief goods, or runs a center that is not listed yet, message me and I will add it.
